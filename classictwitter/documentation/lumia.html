flashing original firmware

This is not needed, but, if you prefer to do a full system reset before flashing a custom rom, then you can flash the original firmware.

Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Open C:\ProgramData\Nokia\Packages
  2. Create a "Products" folder (if it doesn't exist yet) and then a folder with your phone model at C:\ProgramData\Nokia\Packages
    (It should look like this: (e.g. C:\ProgramData\Nokia\Packages\Products\RM-809)
  3. Move your device's flash files (firmware) into that folder.
  4. Power off your phone and unplug from any USB.
  5. Open C:\Program Files (x86)\Nokia\Nokia Care Suite\Product Support Tool For Store 5.0\ProductSupportToolForStore.exe
  6. Click File (topbar), Then Open Product.
  7. Insert your model (e.g. RM-809) on the Filter.
  8. Click Programming, then Recovery.
  9. On the Properties, There should be your Lumia's, respectively, Code, Variant Name, Version. (The loaders need to match.)
  10. Click Start, don't plug your phone yet.
  11. After a few seconds, a window should appear saying that device has lost connection, Holding the volume up (+) button on your phone, quiclky plug your phone to the USB cable connected to your PC.
  12. After a bit, you should feel a slight vibration, meaning you're sucessful.
  13. The window should saying "Flashing" and some other stuff. Wait until the process finishes. (If your Lumia vibrated but nothing changed on PC, means you need to install correctly your driver.)
  14. Congrats, you've successfully flashed your phone's original firmware.

updating to WP7.8

Note: You can just install a custom rom, which is more feature packed, but if you just want to update to WP7.8 after flashing original firmware, or not.

Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Plug your phone to the USB.
  2. Open SevenEighter, and click Install WP7.8.
  3. Follow the instructions on-screen.

flashing qualcomm loaders

Note: Again, not all Qualcomm loaders of all devices in the list will be avaliable in the archive, and will get added in the future.
Why is this needed?

Because it allows communication between NSS Pro & the phone, allowing you to flash custom roms on your phone.

Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Open NSS Pro as a administrator.
  2. Click on the flashing tab, and then WP7 Tools.
  3. Now, put the qualcomm loaders files on the NSSPro directory, \loaders\special\wp7.
  4. After that, click install and follow the instructions on-screen.

installing a custom rom

Note: Again, not all Qualcomm loaders of all devices in the list will be avaliable in the archive, and will get added in the future.
Why is this needed?

Because it allows communication between NSS Pro & the phone, allowing you to flash custom roms on your phone.

Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Open NSS Pro as a administrator.
  2. Click on the flashing tab, and then WP7 Tools.
  3. Click "OS File" and open your os-new.nb (or the file with the biggest size with a .nb extension/format) file (The file in the selected ROM you want to flash).
  4. Click Write OS, and wait for the operation to be complete. If any instructions are shown, follow them.

rooting the custom rom

Note: Not all custom roms are compatible with the WMDC method. If any app related to WDMC isn't present, then it probably isn't supported. Try the WP7USBStorageEnabler method instead.
Why is this needed?

Because it allows you to transfer files, such as XAPs (apps), to your phone.

Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Power on your phone and plug your device normally.
  2. With Windows Mobile Device Center and Zune installed, it should open automatically. If not, open them manually.
  3. Setup your device if you didn't already.
  4. On your explorer tab, there should be a laptop or your device logo and name as a device.

Or, if that didn't work:

  1. Open WP7USBStorageEnabler.
  2. Connect your phone to the USB, on.
  3. Click Enable, then replug the device in the USB.
  4. On your explorer tab, there should be a MP3 Player or your device logo and name as a device.

installing apps+games (xaps)

Note: Before continuing, know that the Lumiatrix ROM may have problems when trying to install a XAP.

There is 2 methods in doing this. Method 1:

Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Open the start menu (PC) and look for "Application Deployment" on the WP8 SDK Lite/WP7 SDK folder.
  2. With your phone plugged in the USB, select the XAP you want to deploy.
  3. Click Deploy and wait. If it returns a error, it probably is corruped, or made for a newer version of WP.

Or:

  1. With WMDC opened (or WP7USBStorageEnabler enabled) and device connected, click "Open Phone files" (or open your phone directory in explorer).
  2. Go to \, then My Documents and create a new folder.
  3. Then, copy and paste your XAPs to the folder you just created.
  4. On your phone, open "XAP Deployer", if it isn't there, look at the settings.
  5. Click the folder icon at the appbar.
  6. Select the XAP you want to install.
  7. Click Install XAP, and wait for the process to end. If it returns a error, it probably is corruped, or made for a newer version of WP.
